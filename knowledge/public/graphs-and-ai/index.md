# Graphs and AI: utility-first paper index

This is the utility-first reading order for [the complete annotated catalog](papers.md).
It preserves every retained paper at the **2026-08-14** cutoff and exposes the
downloadable arXiv PDF as a separate direct link.

Priority is editorial rather than a claim of universal quality: **Start here** is a curated cross-topic path, **Current strong** is high-value 2026 evidence, and **Foundation** is durable prior work.
Within each tier, broader decision value, stronger evaluation, and current
operational relevance come before narrower contributions.
