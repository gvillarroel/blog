# Skills: utility-first paper index

This is the utility-first reading order for [the complete annotated catalog](skills.md).
It preserves every retained paper at the **2026-08-13** cutoff and exposes the
downloadable arXiv PDF as a separate direct link.

Priority is editorial rather than a claim of universal quality: **Start here** is the shortest high-value path, **Essential** is grade-A evidence, **Useful** is narrower or provisional grade-B evidence, and **Reference** is survey or specification material.
Within each tier, broader decision value, stronger evaluation, and current
operational relevance come before narrower contributions.
